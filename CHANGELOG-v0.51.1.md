# HGN v0.51.1

## AI Desk expansion
- Added newsroom, Guide, event, site-check, sales, billing follow-up and renewal item types.
- Added type filters, manual intake form, reviewer tracking and clearer source/agent details.
- Nothing publishes or sends messages automatically.

## Advertising Operations foundation
- Added a focused four-number Operations dashboard.
- Added Customers, Billing Queue and Sales Desk admin pages.
- Added manual invoice-sent, paid, overdue and follow-up tracking.
- Kept advertising production separate from billing.
