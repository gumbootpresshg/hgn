# HGN v0.65.5 - Notices Admin

- Adds **Admin → Community → Notices**.
- Staff can create, edit, publish, archive and delete notices directly.
- Adds notice type, organization, start/expiry dates, link, attachment URL and featured status.
- Public Notices now hide expired notices and prioritize featured/current items.
- Paid public-notice submission remains available for reader/business bookings.
