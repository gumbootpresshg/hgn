# HGN v0.48.5 - Homepage Editorial Flow

## Homepage layout

- Filled the unused space beneath the lead image with two compact supporting story links.
- Kept the lead text, image, and opinion rail as independent editorial columns so one tall column no longer creates a visually empty centre.
- Reassigned the four-story strip to fresh secondary stories rather than repeating stories already used beneath the lead image.
- Converted the More Local News area to a two-column masonry-style flow so shorter cards no longer leave matching blank gaps beside taller cards.
- Increased the More Local News pool from eight to ten stories to give the main column enough depth beside the events and poll rail.
- Kept the middle advertisement inside the editorial flow while preventing it from splitting across columns.

## Preserved

- Option 1 masthead and 2024 founding year.
- v0.48.4 headline sizing and sticky navigation improvements.
- Existing routes, Supabase queries, article selection logic, events, polls, advertising, marketplace, and admin tools.

No Supabase migration is required.
