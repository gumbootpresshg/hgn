# HGN v0.65.4 — News Distribution & Google News Readiness

- Added a publisher-facing News Distribution desk.
- Kept the Google News sitemap and strengthened the main sitemap with writer and managed-page URLs.
- Added RSS feeds for all articles, News, Opinion and Sports plus an Atom feed.
- Added IndexNow support with automatic notification after an article is published or updated.
- Added an IndexNow key endpoint for participating search engines.
- Added optional Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster meta-verification environment settings.
- Strengthened NewsArticle structured data by linking article authors to HGN writer profile pages.
- Added Person structured data and canonical metadata to writer profile pages.
- Added WebSite/SearchAction and stronger NewsMediaOrganization identity metadata.
- Reworked the SEO Readiness Desk to inspect the actual article fields used by the current editor.
- Added News Distribution to Publisher Settings and the Platform admin workspace.

No Supabase migration is required.
