# HGN v0.53.1 - Newspaper Newsletter Theme

## Newsletter design
- Rebuilt newsletter email as a clean newspaper-style edition.
- Text masthead replaces the large logo in the header.
- Added lead-story treatment, strong typography, section rules, story imagery and compact article rows.
- Added a small HGN seal only in the footer.
- Improved mobile email layout for narrow screens.

## Events
- Removes duplicate events with the same title and date.
- Adds readable dates, times, locations, communities and descriptions.
- Applies the event repair to manual and automatic newsletter builds.

## Included foundation
- Retains the v0.53.0 tourist guide, map, Guide Keeper automation and reporting hub.

No Supabase migration is required for this release.
