# Deploy HGN v0.67.42

No Supabase migration is required.

After deploying, open **Admin → Authors**, choose a writer and use **Author portrait** to upload the original image, position it, then select **Use this portrait**. The photo saves directly to that author's profile.

Complete each writer's role, short bio, full public bio and public email there as well. Those details are editorial facts and are deliberately not filled automatically.
